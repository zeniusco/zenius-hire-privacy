Zenius Hire - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Zenius Hire ("the extension") is a Chrome extension that helps recruiters source candidates, evaluate them with AI and send approved candidates to Zoho Recruit. This policy explains what data the extension handles and where it goes.

Who we are

The extension is published by Zenius (zenius.co). Contact: rohit@zenius.co

No developer-run servers

We do not operate a server that receives your data. The extension has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and we do not collect, see or store the data it handles. Everything described below stays in your browser or goes directly to services you configured yourself.

What the extension handles

  • Candidate information shown on the sites you use it on (Naukri Resdex, LinkedIn job applicants, Internshala): name, headline, location, skills, work history, expected and current salary, contact details where the site shows them, screening answers, and resumes.
  • Your settings and credentials: project definitions, prompts, and the keys or tokens you enter for Zoho Recruit, Supabase and your AI provider.
  • Run logs and progress, so a run can continue after a page change.

Where that data goes

  1. Your browser. Settings, run progress and logs are kept in the extension's local browser storage on your computer.
  2. Your AI provider (Anthropic, OpenRouter, Azure OpenAI or AWS Bedrock - whichever you configure). Candidate details and resume text are sent to it so it can evaluate the candidate. Its own privacy terms apply.
  3. Zoho Recruit, when you approve a candidate: their details, resume and a note are sent to your Zoho Recruit account.
  4. Your Supabase project. Candidate records and decisions are written to the database you configure so the same person is not reviewed twice.

We do not sell this data, do not transfer it to anyone else, and do not use it for advertising, credit or lending decisions, or any purpose unrelated to the recruiting features above.

Permissions

The extension asks for browser permissions (tabs, scripting, storage, alarms, clipboard, debugger and others) only to operate the supported sites and carry out recruiting steps you start. They are not used to browse your history or to read pages outside the supported sites.

Security and retention

Credentials you enter are stored in your browser's extension storage. Data in Zoho, Supabase and your AI provider is retained under the settings and policies of those services and your own account. Removing the extension deletes its local data. Delete records in Zoho or Supabase to remove them there.

Candidates' rights

Candidate data is processed on behalf of the recruiting team using the extension. Requests from candidates to access or delete their data should go to that team, who control the Zoho and Supabase records.

Children

The extension is not intended for use by anyone under 18 and does not knowingly process data about children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
